# Dr. Grewal demo content sources

This file records the public information and image provenance used in the Version 1 approval demonstration. Public listings were treated as reference material, not as permission to reproduce unsupported claims.

## Business information

- https://www.grewalhomeoremedies.com/ — business name, Mohali location, public catalogue names and general product groupings.
- https://www.grewalhomeoremedies.com/profile.html — establishment year (1973), Mohali address and public product portfolio.
- https://www.grewalhomeoremedies.com/homeopathic-veterinary-medicines.html — product names, public product photographs and source descriptions for G Appet, G Uddar, G Eliksir, G Petworm and G Dermi.
- https://www.grewalhomeoremedies.com/duplicate.html — G Liv name and public product photograph.

The demo paraphrases only neutral catalogue information. Claims about cures, guaranteed outcomes, safety, superiority, certification, side effects and milk yield were intentionally excluded.

## Product image provenance

The six product images are copies of photographs displayed on the business's own public IndiaMART-managed catalogue pages above. They are included only in this private approval prototype to demonstrate the proposed catalogue. Dr. Grewal must confirm ownership/permission and supply approved high-resolution originals before production.
